Clearing Asteroids

You start heading to the weapons room now that the reactor meltdown has been stopped. You arrive at Weapons and use the laser cannon to shoot the debris around the ship, clearing as many asteroids as you can.

Now that you have finished all your tasks, You decide to head over to Security to check the cameras to see if you can find any dead bodies.

On your way there the lights turn off.

It must be another sabotage. You start heading over to electrical to fix the lights. It is impossible to see anything around you. When you arrive at electrical, you open up the fuse box and see that all the switches have been turned off. You quickly toggle them all back on, but it's too late.

You hear the meeting alarm again.


You arrive at the cafeteria. This time, Blue has called the meeting.

Yellow is gone and Purple has some explaining to do.

We each take turns explaining our whereabouts since the last meeting. Everyone's explanations seem to make sense. Blue points out that Purple was the one who falsely accused Red and that she's the reason Red is now dead.

Green and Cyan also voted for Red, you can't blame that all on me!

You tell everyone that you trust Blue because she helped you fix the reactor and that she has had chances to kill you but hasn't. Blue calls for a vote.

About the game

Among Us is a multiplayer game that has skyrocketed in popularity in recent weeks. It was developed by Innersloth and was released in 2018. It is a social deduction party game that is best played with friends.

How to play

You and your fellow crewmates must prep a spaceship for departure by doing various tasks throughout the ship. However, one of your crewmates is not what they seem. You must beware the Imposter, a creature that you and your crew knows has replaced one of the members of the ship and is trying to kill the entire crew. You and your fellow crewmates can defeat the imposter by completing all your tasks or discovering who the imposter is and throwing it out of the airlock. A suspected imposter will only be ejected off the ship by a majority vote. The Imposter can sabotage various ship functions in order to create chaos and distraction that will make it easier for it to kill and get away with it. If at any point a body is discovered, a meeting will be called where you and the crew can discuss who the Imposter might be.

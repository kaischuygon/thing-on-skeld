Lets get started!

You are Cyan, a crewmate aboard The Skeld

The Skeld is a spaceship on its way to back to Earth after a deep-space exploration mission. The ship is heavily damaged because of an encounter with an alien race who you know is imitating one of your crew members. You and the rest of your crew have agreed to split up in order to do the tasks as quickly as possible, even though it will make it easier for the imposter to pick you off one by one. Luckily, due to your extensive research on the alien species, you know that the stowaway on your ship can only kill every couple minutes, which should be enough time for someone to find the body and call a meeting before the imposter kills anyone else. You can only take off your helmet in the meeting room so you will not be able to communicate while you are out doing tasks.
